01

Insurance claims reserving with the chain ladder

General insurance · ChainLadder · Mack model · Bootstrap

Using the RAA general liability triangle (1981–1990), I projected each accident year's claims to their final cost with the chain ladder. I then measured the uncertainty in the reserve with Mack standard errors and a 5,000-simulation over-dispersed Poisson bootstrap.

02

Motor insurance pricing with GLMs

Non-life pricing · Poisson GLM · Gamma GLM · 678,000 policies

On the French motor third-party liability dataset, a Poisson GLM predicts how often each driver claims and a Gamma GLM predicts how much each claim costs. Multiplying the two gives a risk-based pure premium for every policy. Both models are validated on a 20% held-out test set.

03

Irish life expectancy and mortality

Life insurance · CSO Irish Life Tables No. 17 · Gompertz–Makeham · Annuities

From the CSO's official 2015–2017 life table, I built full life tables by sex, fitted the Gompertz and Makeham laws of mortality by Poisson maximum likelihood, and priced term life cover and lifetime pensions from first principles.
